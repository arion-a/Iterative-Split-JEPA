## C3 Columbia confound check: kinetic, potential, amplitude (registered 2026-10-21, before the run; owner-requested; `configs/columbia_confound.yaml`)
**Question.** Is the recovered coordinate energy-like, or a purely kinetic, positional or amplitude quantity? **Run.** The C2 pipelines retrained from the same seeds and recipe (reproduction check: main naming within 0.005 of C2), test videos only, valid frames. |Spearman| of psi with tracked **total**, **kinetic**, **potential** energy and a local **amplitude** (sqrt of the summed variances of sin/cos of both tracked angles over a ±30-frame window); the flat arm for comparison; partial Spearman of psi with total energy given amplitude, and with amplitude given total energy (informational).
**Prediction (Claude's).** psi's |Spearman| with total energy exceeds its |Spearman| with kinetic and with potential energy on every seed. **Decision rule:** confirmed if that holds on all three seeds and the reproduction check passes; refuted otherwise. Amplitude carries no pass/fail: for a pendulum amplitude and energy are physically tied, so a high correlation with both is expected; the partial correlations say which survives. Claude's expectation: total beats both components; amplitude correlates about as high as total energy, and the partials will not cleanly separate them. Not tuned after the result.

### C3 outcome (2026-10-21), `results/columbia_confound/*.json`: **CONFIRMED** (as registered)
Reproduction of the C2 main arm exact (difference 0.0002 on every seed; 11361 valid test frames). psi's |Spearman| on the 20 test videos, per seed: total energy [0.905, 0.915, 0.913], kinetic [0.509, 0.517, 0.514], potential [0.543, 0.549, 0.555], amplitude [0.908, 0.912, 0.908]. Total energy exceeds both components on every seed (by about 0.36-0.40 over kinetic and 0.35-0.37 over potential).
Informational: flat arm total / kinetic / potential / amplitude on seed 0: 0.73 / 0.57 / 0.281 / 0.696. Partial Spearman of psi with total energy given amplitude [0.484, 0.528, 0.527], with amplitude given total energy [0.509, 0.512, 0.492]; energy and amplitude correlate [0.898, 0.898, 0.898] with each other.
**Reading.** The coordinate is not a kinetic or a positional quantity (about 0.5 with each); it tracks total energy and local swing amplitude about equally (0.91 each) and these two are tied to each other (0.90), so the data cannot separate "energy" from "amplitude": after removing either, about 0.5 remains with the other. That is what a pendulum predicts (amplitude fixes energy), so this check supports "energy-like slow coordinate" and does not support "energy as opposed to amplitude". Claude's expectation held (total beats both components; amplitude as high as energy; partials do not separate them).
