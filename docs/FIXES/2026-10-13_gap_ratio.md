# Fix brief: rung-2 gap ratio in B2 (clean world)

Status 2026-10-14. Decisions: D10a, D11 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Predictions: E12, E13, E14 in `docs/EVIDENCE.md` (earlier labels E10a/b/c). Results: `results/stage_b2_purify/`, `results/stage_b2_noop/`, `results/diagnostics/tier2/`.

## 1. The problem
In the clean B2 world (sigma_cat 0.1) the rung-2 gap ratio is 2.7 / 2.7 / 3.9 per seed (gate: above 10), while the B1 arm on the same encoders gets 28.7 / 31.0 / 22.2. The splitting itself works: given correct pairs the spectrum has a cliff.

## 2. Mechanism
Rung 2 takes the difference moment M of matched pairs inside the rung-1 invariant content H_1. Valid pairs share their category, so their differences carry no energy in the category (invariant) directions: those are the bottom eigendirections. A fraction rho of the clustered pairs is mis-grouped (different categories). Those pairs carry energy exactly in the bottom directions, so M = M_good + rho M_bad. That energy raises the (d_2+1)-th eigenvalue and flattens the cliff: the gap ratio falls even though the variant directions are found. The clustering that feeds the pairs finds 5 clusters instead of 6 on all three seeds, so pair validity is 0.83 and rho is about 0.17.

## 3. Tier 1: purification (canonical, hard trim)
Fit M inside H_1; score every pair by its energy in the bottom (invariant) eigendirections; drop pairs scoring over 4 x the median; re-fit; repeat until the kept set stops changing or 5 iterations. No labels and no validity estimate are used. Constants were fixed before the run (D10a).
Result (E12, 3 seeds): pair validity 0.83 -> 1.00 (0.997 on seed 2) at unchanged cluster purity; 82% of pairs kept, 98-99% of valid pairs kept, 98.5-100% of invalid pairs dropped; rung-2 gap per seed 2.7 -> 19.2, 2.7 -> 22.2, 3.9 -> 17.6; share of the B1-B2 deficit recovered 0.68.

## 4. Tier 4: within-H_1 whitening (demoted)
Idea: rescale H_1 coordinates so no direction counts more for scale reasons. Registered treatment was Tier 1 + Tier 4 (gap 17.0 / 19.5 / 19.5, 0.64 of the deficit recovered). It added nothing over Tier 1 (a21_fro 0.078 against 0.051). Reason, noted by Claude: after the global ZCA whitening, any orthonormal subspace of the population already has identity covariance, so the extra whitening is close to a no-op. Now off by default and kept only for non-whitened pipelines (D11).

## 8. Tier 2 diagnosis (analysis only; unsupervised clusterer input; true categories used only to name which categories merge)
Why the sixth category is missed, per seed (s0 / s1 / s2):
* Not size: the six categories have 9,800-10,200 samples each of the 60,000.
* Geometry: in the 8-dimensional rung-1 invariant output the clusterer sees, the closest pair of category centres is 2.17 / 2.01 / 1.73 apart while the mean within-category radius is about 1.9, because identity variation inside each category is large. The closest pair is about one radius apart (ratio 1.13 / 1.05 / 0.90).
* The merged pair is the closest pair of centres on every seed (categories 4+5, 0+2, 2+5).
* Model selection, not subsampling noise: silhouette prefers 5 over 6 in 17 / 20 / 20 of 20 different 3,000-point subsamples (mean difference k6 minus k5: -0.004 / -0.014 / -0.022; on 12,000 points 0.288 vs 0.281, 0.292 vs 0.281, 0.298 vs 0.273). Seed 0 is close to a tie.
* The categories are separable at the right count: k-means with k=6 and 10 restarts reaches purity 0.999 / 1.000 on seeds 0 and 1.
* Initialisation matters on one seed: seed 2 reaches only 0.901 at k=6 even with 10 restarts (single initialisations: median 0.833, none perfect), and seeds 0 and 1 reach a perfect partition from 6 / 20 and 8 / 20 single initialisations.
Takeaway: the cause is the closest category pair sitting about one cluster radius apart in the clusterer's input, which makes silhouette favour merging them; seed 2 also shows k-means local minima at the right count. Not yet tested: whether clustering only the category directions, or another count-selection rule, fixes it. A rebuild waits (D11) and must stay fully unsupervised (no generator labels in construction).
