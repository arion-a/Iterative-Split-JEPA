# Plan: closing the gaps between "works on a representation that contains the structure" and "works in practice", especially for deeper ladders

Written 2026-10-21 at the owner's request. **Planning document only: nothing here has been registered or run.** Each phase registers its predictions in `docs/EVIDENCE.md` (additions only) and freezes its criteria by hash BEFORE its runs, as before; the numbers below marked "draft" are proposals, fixed only at registration. Same rules as the earlier plans: no tuning to fit, refuted results are results, a check after every step and a gate after every phase, a supervisor during runs, anything outside the plan goes to "Noted, not started".

## 0. What can honestly be aimed at
"Works in all cases" cannot be established by any finite set of tests. What can be established is: (1) the ladder works whenever stated preconditions hold (the encoder kept the content; correspondences are right; the blocks are separable), at the depths tested; and (2) when a precondition fails, the pipeline detects it and says so, instead of silently removing directions and damaging later rungs. Phases G1-G6 aim at those two things, with limits located where they exist.

### Phase G4-K: fix the cluster-count selection (the E18 refutation)
**Diagnosis (exploratory, not registered; true latents, 3 seeds x sigma_cat 0.1 / 0.4 / 0.6; `experiments/diag_k_selection_space.py`, output in `results/diagnostics/k_selection/output.txt`).** The selector itself is not the problem; the space it runs in is. In the B2 world each of the 6 categories has its own 20 identity centres, so the 8-dimensional rung-1 invariant output holds 6 x 20 = 120 tight blobs and the cluster count read from the Laplacian eigengap in that space is a count of identity-and-category blobs: K = 9, 12, 12, 10, 8, 9, 12, 2, 7 (K = 6 in 0 of 9; this reproduces the E18 pattern). In the category block alone (oracle) the same selector with the same constants gives K = 6 in 9 of 9. The category block is also the slowest part of the dynamics (speed 0.95 against 0.7 for identity), and the 4 slowest directions of the adjacent-frame change, found without labels, lie 2.2-2.8 deg from the category block and give K = 6 in 9 of 9. So K should be selected in the slow subspace of the invariant output, not in the whole of it. This was shown with true latents; with a trained encoder it is untested.
**Solution.** Keep the E18 selector and its frozen constants (no tuning); change the space: (i) take the rung-1 invariant output; (ii) estimate the second moment of the adjacent-frame change inside it; (iii) take its slowest directions, their number set by the largest gap in that spectrum (existing `select_block_count`; report "no slow block" if none); (iv) run `spectral_k` there; (v) k-means at that K in the same subspace; then pairs inside clusters and Tier 1 as before. Limit: it needs time order and a speed difference between the category block and the identity block; without them it abstains.

Roughly 150 runs in total (G1 about 40, G2 about 30, G3 about 45, G4 about 30, G6 about 20) plus the G5 re-analysis; at the observed speed on 4 CPU cores that is on the order of 8-12 hours of compute (not measured for the new settings).
