What the method does, as dots in space

A video frame becomes a vector: one dot in a space with d coordinates (here d = 16 to 24). The method never looks at pixels again; it only moves and cuts this cloud of dots.

1. The whole pipeline in one line

2. One split, geometrically

A. Matched pairs Grey: all dots. Orange: a pair of twins. They agree on the slow layers (same height) and differ in the fast one (move sideways).
B. Differences of pairs Subtract each pair. The cloud of differences is an ellipsoid: long axes = directions that changed = the variant block V1; short axes = directions that never changed.
C. Cut at the gap Keep the short axes (H1), throw away the long ones (V1). The dots flatten onto the slow directions. Gap = λ(last long) / λ(first short). Big gap = the block really exists; gap ≈ 1 = nothing to cut.

3. Repeating: the ladder

Each rung needs only its own matched pairs and one eigendecomposition. Nothing is trained after the encoder.

4. What comes out

A set of perpendicular subspaces, ordered fast to slow, each one holding one layer: V1 = the layer that changes most between pairs, … core = what never changes. In the synthetic worlds each block can be read back to its planted layer.

5. What is common to all the tests

  1. The pairs define the layers. Which layer you get is decided entirely by what the two dots in a pair share. Speed of change is not needed (equal speeds: ladder 1°, one-shot temporal split 80°).
  2. The gap tells you if it worked. A big gap means a real block; a gap near 1 means the pairs or the embedding broke the assumption.
  3. Every failure is a broken assumption, not a broken eigen-cut: pairs not exact (clusters), a layer smeared nonlinearly through the embedding (strong bend), information lost by the encoder (generic MLP), or no matching information at all (robot images).
  4. Errors accumulate down the ladder: each rung works inside what the previous one left, so a small leak at rung 1 becomes a bigger one at rung 4.

Not shown: any benefit for prediction, or anything on real video. The mechanism is validated where the pairs are given and the bend is mild.
