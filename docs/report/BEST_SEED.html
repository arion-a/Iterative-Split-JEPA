The best seed, in detail

E71a, K = 11 layers (10 rungs), coupling strength 0.5, arm A (joint nested loss), seed 284. W = 1, 82,500 steps, 2.6 h on the GPU.
How it was chosen, and what that means. Best = the run with the largest share of clean rungs among the 15 E71a runs: 5 of 10 rungs clean (next best: 6 of 12, K = 13, seed 280). It was picked after looking at all runs, so it is the most favourable one, not a typical one; the other four seeds at this K are in the last table. Even this run does not pass the registered test (all rungs clean).

Summary of this run

  • Clean slices: 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 (speeds 0.82 to 0.96): presence 0.90 to 0.99, no leakage, residual at most 0.023. Slice 6 (speed 0.82) only just passes: presence 0.901.
  • Not clean: slices 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (speeds 0.20 to 0.76): presence 0.21 to 0.76, short of the 0.9 line by 0.14 to 0.69. Slice 1 also has residual 0.059 against the 0.05 limit.
  • Health: smallest output-covariance eigenvalue 0.84 (needs 0.5): not collapsed.
  • Prediction loss: 0.481, against 0.412 for a faithful embedding (excess +0.069).
  • Whole-latent readout: global R-squared 0.667 (untrained, same seed: 0.495).

2. Invariance and leakage

Residual: how far the slices slower than rung k differ between the two members of a rung-k pair (0 = identical, about 2 = unrelated). Leakage: the largest R-squared with which any faster layer can be read from the slice.

The counts match the expected 4k at rungs 2 to 10, and are 5 at rung 1 (one more than 4). The drop from the large to the small group is a factor of 22 to 97 at every rung. So the geometry of the pair differences is nested as intended, even for the fast layers whose slices are not readable. A caution: the stored ladder measure for the same embedding (eigen-split angle to the true block, a linear comparison) reads 56 to 90 degrees and gap ratio about 1.0 at every rung, i.e. it reports no recovered block. I have not reconciled the two readings; the angle compares linear subspaces of a nonlinear embedding, so it is the likelier one to understate recovery, but that is a guess, not a result.
