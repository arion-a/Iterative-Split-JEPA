E71b: deep worlds at the stronger coupling (strength 1)

30 runs: 11, 13 and 16 layers (10, 12, 15 rungs), nested loss (arm A) and an untrained control, 5 new seeds (300 to 304). Run on an RTX 4090 pod, mean time per trained run 1.8 h / 2.3 h / 3.1 h. All numbers come from results/nested_pod_e71b. Registered in docs/EVIDENCE.md before any run (owner override of the original condition; coupling chosen by the owner).

1. Result in plain words

  • Speeds are recovered in the right order, but read too low. For the slow layers (speed 0.86 and above) the recovered speed is below the true one on every seed: mean error -0.041 / -0.046 / -0.060 at 11 / 13 / 16 layers (at coupling 0.5, E72: -0.012 / -0.012 / -0.011). The seeds agree with each other (standard deviation about 0.003), so the error is a consistent offset, not noise.
  • In terms of how fast the layer changes (1 − speed) the recovered rate is 55% / 61% / 83% too large (E72: about 15%).
  • The slow rungs are no longer cleanly invariant: the invariance residual of the slow rungs reaches 0.111 / 0.127 / 0.181 (limit 0.05) and slow-layer presence falls to 0.895 / 0.887 / 0.867 (needs 0.9).
  • Both registered accuracy lines fail on every seed (table below); the order line holds on every seed.

Invariance residual by rung (mean over 5 seeds). At coupling 0.5 the slow rungs stay near 0.02; at coupling 1 they rise to 0.07 to 0.18, above the 0.05 limit, most at 16 layers.

4. The transformations, with equations

Notation: K layers of 4 numbers, d = 4K = 44 / 52 / 64; z = [z1 | ... | zK] hidden numbers (layer 1 fastest); coupling strength s = 1; batch N = 256. Equation numbers refer to NESTED_TECHNICAL.pdf.

(1) Hidden numbers over timeworlds.py: BlockWorld
zt+1,b = ρb zt,b + √(1 − ρb2) εt,b,  ε ~ N(0, I4)
Speeds: 1 − ρb = 0.8 · 0.05(b−1)/(K−1), from ρ1 = 0.2 to ρK = 0.96. Layers with ρ ≥ 0.86 are the 'slow' layers judged here (layers 7 to 11, 8 to 13, 10 to 16).
(2) Observation (strength s = 1)worlds.py: CouplingMap
zR := zR + tanh(zL Wi) (i odd);  zL := zL + tanh(zR Wi) (i even);  Wi = 1 · Qi
x = C1(z) ∈ Rd; twice the coupling of E72 (s = 0.5), so each tanh term moves the numbers twice as far.
(3) Matched pair of rung kworlds.py: correspondence_pairs
vb = ub for b > k;  vb ~ N(0, I4) fresh for b ≤ k
Layers slower than k identical in u and v.
(4) Encoderencoder.py
h = W3 GELU(W2 GELU(W1 x)),  h = [h1 | ... | hK]
d → 16K → 16K → d (hidden width 176 / 208 / 256).
(5) Loss for one stepencoder.py: train_encoder
L = Lpred + Σk=1K−1 [ Linv,k + W Lwd,k ] + W Lwh + 0.01 Lvar + 0.04 Lcov
W = 1 (pilot of 10,000 steps: smallest output-covariance eigenvalue 0.775 / 0.745 / 0.688 at 11 / 13 / 16 layers, all at least 0.5). 7,500 K steps = 82,500 / 97,500 / 120,000; AdamW, learning rate 3·10−3, cosine decay in the second half. Terms:
Lpred = (1/N d) Σ ‖ f(ht) − sg(ht+1) ‖2;  Linv,k = mean ( huT − hvT )2, T = slices k+1..K;  Lwd,k = ‖ Cov(huk − hvk) − 2 I4 ‖F2
Lwh = ‖ Cov(h) − Id ‖F2;  Lvar = Σj max(0, 1 − √(Var hj + 10−4));  Lcov = (1/d) Σi≠j Cov(h)ij2

Final predictive loss (mean of 5 seeds, last training step) 0.494 / 0.505 / 0.545, against the reference (1/K) Σ(1 − ρb2) = 0.412 / 0.410 / 0.408 for a faithful embedding.

(7) Presence, leakage, healthstage_b1.py: slice_measures
presenceb = R2(zb from hb);  leakageb = maxc<b R2(zc from hb);  health = λmin(Cov h)
R2 = 1 − Σ(z − ẑ)2 / Σ(z − z̄)2 from a tanh network (64 units, 800 steps), fitted on 15,000 and scored on 5,000 fresh points.
(8) Invariance residualstage_b1.py: invariance_residual
rk = mean ( huT − hvT )2 / meanj∈T Var(huj)
5,000 fresh rung-k pairs; 0 = identical, 2 = unrelated. Clean rung: presence ≥ 0.9, leakage ≤ 0.1, r ≤ 0.05.
(10) Speed read-backstage_b1.py: rho_hat
ρ̂b = mean over the 4 coordinates j of slice b of corr( ht,j, ht+1,j );  error = ρ̂b − ρb
50,000 held-out transition pairs (zt, zt+1) from equation (1), pushed through (2) and (4); each coordinate centred over the pairs; no labels used. For a coordinate that carries one layer, the correlation equals the layer's speed; a coordinate that also mixes in faster layers would read lower, which could explain the low bias (not tested).
Slow-rung criterion S:  health ≥ 0.5,  every slow rung k < K clean,  core presence ≥ 0.9.   Speed line R:  maxslow b |ρ̂b − ρb| ≤ 0.02.   Order:  ρ̂b increasing in b over the slow layers.

5. Limits

  • One new coupling (1); with E72 (0.5) that is two points, not a map of the dependence.
  • Slices are read at fixed positions (layout given); the 0.86 cut was taken from E71a; oracle nested pairs; block sizes given; 5 seeds per cell; synthetic worlds.
  • The possible reason for the low bias given in step 10 (a slice that also mixes in faster layers reads lower) is a reading of the numbers, not tested.
  • Trained encoders were saved: results/nested_pod_e71b/results/nested/e71b/A/K*_s1/enc_seed*.pt.
