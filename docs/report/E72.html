E72: slow layers and their speeds

30 new runs (seeds 290 to 294) at K = 11, 13, 16 layers, coupling strength 0.5, nested loss (arm A) and an untrained control; all numbers from results/nested_pod_e72. Test registered before the run in docs/EVIDENCE.md.
  • The slow layers are recovered again on new seeds, in 12 of 15 trained runs. The registered line needed 4 of 5 at every depth; K = 16 got 3 of 5, short by one seed, and the misses are by 0.003 to 0.011 of presence.
  • The speed of each recovered slice can be read back from the data alone, to within 0.006 to 0.021 on average (largest single error 0.030), and in the right order on all 15 runs. The untrained encoder reads about 0.66 on every slice: no speed information.
  • The estimate is biased low: all 90 slow-slice estimates are below the true speed (mean error -0.0115), more so at the fast end of the slow set.
  • Limits: the slice layout was given, the 0.86 cut came from the earlier E71a runs, one world family, mild coupling.

2. Estimated speed against true speed

Each dot is one slow slice of one trained run (blue K = 11, orange K = 13, red K = 16; 90 dots). Grey dots: the untrained encoder. The green dashed line is a perfect estimate.

How the speed is estimated, from held-out transition pairs (ht, ht+1), no labels:

ρ̂b = mean over the 4 coordinates j of slice b of  Σ ( ht,j − h̄t,j )( ht+1,j − h̄t+1,j ) / √[ Σ ( ht,j − h̄t,j )2 · Σ ( ht+1,j − h̄t+1,j )2 ]
