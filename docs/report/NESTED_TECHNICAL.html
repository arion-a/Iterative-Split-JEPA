Nested loss and speed recovery: technical report

Scope: what was built for the experiments E68, E71a, E72, E71b and E73, how every number is computed from the input data, what was run, and what came out. Synthetic worlds only. 150 trained runs. All numbers are taken from the stored result files.

1. Summary

  • Question. Can a loss alone, without being told which part is which, place hidden layers that change at different speeds into fixed slices of the encoder output, so that the slow layers are kept and their speeds can be read back?
  • Speeds are recovered where the layers are recovered. Small world (true speeds 0.2, 0.7, 0.95), paper recipe: recovered 0.204, 0.695, 0.937 at coupling 0.5 (errors +0.004, -0.005, -0.013) and 0.202, 0.683, 0.908 at coupling 1. Deep worlds (slow layers, speed at least 0.86): mean error -0.0115 at coupling 0.5 and -0.0503 at coupling 1; always below the true speed; order always correct.
  • The loss splits by speed. With 10 to 15 rungs at coupling 0.5, layers slower than about 0.86 are recovered on every seed; faster ones are not.
  • It fails with stronger coupling (the 4-layer hard world at couplings 2 to 8; the speeds at coupling 1 in deep worlds read 0.04 to 0.06 too low) and with a plain MLP trained on the predictive loss alone (small world: blocks not recovered).
  • Registered verdicts: E68 refuted; E71a refuted; E72 criterion refuted by one seed at 15 rungs, speeds and order confirmed; E71b criterion and speeds refuted, order confirmed; E73 refuted by the predictive-loss recipe, paper recipe held (section 5).

3. The method: from input data to every reported number

Notation: K layers of 4 numbers each; d = 4K; z = [z1 | ... | zK] the hidden numbers (layer 1 fastest); N = 256 is the batch size. Each equation names the function that computes it.

3.1 Data

(1) Hidden numbers over timeworlds.py: BlockWorld.transition_pairs
zt+1,b = ρb zt,b + √(1 − ρb2) εt,b,  ε ~ N(0, I4),  zt,b ~ N(0, I4)
z ∈ R4K. Speeds: E68 0.2, 0.5, 0.75, 0.95; E73 0.2, 0.7, 0.95; E71a/E72/E71b 1 − ρb = 0.8 · 0.05(b−1)/(K−1) (0.2 to 0.96).
(2) Observation (fixed, not trained)worlds.py: CouplingMap
four layers i = 1..4 alternating:  zR := zR + tanh(zL Wi) (i odd),  zL := zL + tanh(zR Wi) (i even);  Wi = s Qi
x = Cs(z) ∈ Rd (E73 recipe Q: lifted to 16 numbers). zL, zR = halves of z; Qi fixed random orthogonal; s = coupling strength.
(3) Matched pair of rung kworlds.py: BlockWorld.correspondence_pairs
u ~ N(0, Id);  vb = ub for b > k;  vb ~ N(0, I4) fresh for b ≤ k
Layers slower than k are shared exactly; layers 1 to k are redrawn.

3.2 Encoder and loss

(4) Encoderencoder.py: mlp
h = g(x) = W3 GELU(W2 GELU(W1 x)),  h = [h1 | ... | hK]
Rd → R16K → R16K → Rd; slice b = coordinates 4(b−1) to 4b−1 is meant to hold layer b. Predictor f: same shape.
(5) Training loss (one step)encoder.py: train_encoder
L = Lpred + Σk=1K−1 [ 1·Linv,k + W·Lwd,k ] + W·Lwh + 0.01·Lvar + 0.04·Lcov
Terms below. W = 1 in every world (smaller of {1, 10} whose 10,000-step pilot keeps λmin(Cov h) ≥ 0.5). AdamW, learning rate 3·10−3, cosine decay in the second half; 7,500 K steps (E71a, E72, E71b), 30,000 (E68). Arm B switches the k-term on at step 5,000 (k−1).
Lpred = (1/N d) Σ ‖ f(ht) − sg(ht+1) ‖2  (sg = stop-gradient)
Linv,k = mean ( huT − hvT )2, T = slices k+1..K;   Lwd,k = ‖ Cov(huk − hvk) − 2 I4 ‖F2
Lwh = ‖ Cov(h) − Id ‖F2;   Lvar = Σj max(0, 1 − √(Var hj + 10−4));   Lcov = (1/d) Σi≠j Cov(h)ij2

Which slices each term acts on (example K = 6, k = 2 and 4). Linv,k is what makes the slower slices agree across a pair; Lwd,k keeps slice k from collapsing; the others spread the whole output.

Which experiments use (5). E68, E71a, E72 and E71b train with (5). E73 does not: recipe P (the paper recipe, mirror encoder) trains with an alignment loss (mean squared distance of ht and ht+1 from their mean) plus 10−6·SIGReg; recipe Q (generic MLP) trains with Lpred + 0.01·Lvar + 0.04·Lcov only (no pair terms). In E73 the blocks come from the closed-form eigen-split (11), not from slices.

(6) Reference value of the predictive lossreport only
Lpredref = (1/K) Σb ( 1 − ρb2 )
Best one-step prediction when every layer is kept and outputs have unit variance: 0.561 (E68 speeds); 0.412, 0.410, 0.408 for K = 11, 13, 16. Approximate floor.

3.3 Read-outs on fresh data (encoder frozen)

20,000 fresh z (15,000 to fit read-outs, 5,000 to score), raw output h without whitening.

(7) Presence and leakagestage_b1.py: slice_measures; audits.py: mlp_r2
R2(zc from ha) = 1 − Σ(zc − ẑc)2 / Σ(zc − mean zc)2;  presenceb = R2(zb from hb);  leakageb = maxc<b R2(zc from hb)
ẑ from a tanh network (64 units, 800 Adam steps, batch 1,024, standardized inputs and targets).
(8) Invariance residualstage_b1.py: invariance_residual
rk = mean ( huT − hvT )2 / meanj∈T Var(huj)
5,000 fresh rung-k pairs. 0 = identical; two unrelated points give 2.
(9) Health and the clean rulesanalyse_e68_e71.py: rung_clean; analyse_e72.py
health = λmin( Cov(h) ).  Rung k clean: presencek ≥ 0.9, leakagek ≤ 0.1 (k ≥ 2), rk ≤ 0.05.
Seed all-rungs-clean: every rung clean, health ≥ 0.5 (core slice K: presence only). Slow-rung criterion (E72, E71b): the same for layers with ρ ≥ 0.86 only.
(10) Speed read-backstage_b1.py: rho_hat, block_rho_hat
ρ̂b = mean over the 4 coordinates j of slice b of corr( ht,j, ht+1,j )  (each coordinate centred over the pairs)
50,000 held-out transition pairs. Error = ρ̂ − ρ. Deep worlds: read from the slice b. Small world: read from the eigen-split blocks: ρ̂block = mean over the block's directions β of corr( rt·β, rt+1·β ), r = C−1/2(h − mean h).

3.4 The closed-form eigen-split (small world, E73)

(11) Pair-difference moment and blocksengine.py: fit_whitener, difference_moment, fit_rung
D = r(u) − r(v);  Mk = Hk−1T [ (1/n) Σ D DT ] Hk−1;  Vk = top dk eigenvectors of Mk,  Hk = the rest
30,000 rung-k pairs, ZCA whitening from 30,000 points; H0 = I. Directions the pair changes in have eigenvalue about 2; directions it leaves alone near 0. The last H is the core block.

Real spectra (paper recipe, coupling 0.5, seed 310). At rung 1 four eigenvalues near 2 (texture), then values 0.009 to 0.047; at rung 2, inside what is left, again four (identity).

7. Reproduction

  • Setup: PYTHONPATH=src; seeds as in section 4; one thread per process (OMP_NUM_THREADS=1).
  • Deep and hard worlds: python experiments/run_nested_loss.py e68|e71|e72|e71b --arm A|B|U --strength S --depth K --seeds ... --device cuda; the pod queue is experiments/nested_sup.py [e72|e71b].
  • Small world: python experiments/run_e73.py --recipe P|Q --strength 0.5|1.
  • Verdicts: python experiments/analyse_e68_e71.py ROOT, analyse_e72.py ROOT [e71b 1], analyse_e73.py results/e73. Reports: experiments/make_technical_report.py.
  • Trained encoders were saved for E72, E71b and E73 (enc_seed*.pt); not for E68 and E71a.

8. Limits, corrections and open points

  • Oracle and given structure: matched pairs are exact (generator-made); block sizes are given; slices are read at fixed positions, so the speed test shows that a recovered slice's speed can be read back, not that the layout was found from data alone.
  • Thresholds are mine: 0.9, 0.1, 0.05, the 0.86 speed cut (taken from E71a and reused in E72, E71b), 0.02 and 0.03 error limits, and the prediction levels; they were written before each run, but are not derived.
  • Scope: one world family; 5 seeds per cell; coupling 0.5 and 1 in the deep worlds (two points, not a map); real images not tested.
  • Overrides and corrections on record: E71b ran at the owner's request although its original condition (E68 line (b)) failed, at the owner-chosen coupling 1; the E73 outcome entry's "0.002 to 0.013" mixed a per-seed minimum with a mean (corrected by an appended erratum); E72 and E71b read the 0.86 cut from E71a.
  • Open: why the speed read-back is always low (it looks like a consistent offset; a correction was not tested); why the predictive-loss MLP gives no recoverable blocks in the small world (the angles point to the encoder, not tested); in the best E71a seed the pair-difference spectrum shows the expected 4k large eigenvalues at every rung while the stored linear-subspace angle reads 56 to 90 degrees (not reconciled).
