# PART I — VISION AND EXPLORATORY IDEAS
Items are named by epistemic status: a THESIS is an argued conceptual
claim; a MECHANISM is a designed procedure (specified, testable); an
EXAMPLE is a worked illustration; a CONVENTION is a naming or reporting
standard. Proved mathematics (Part II) uses Lemma/Theorem; open formal
claims use Conjecture (C-series).

### Theses

T1 (abstraction is computable). Abstraction is a nested chain of
partitions, induced by question-functions selected by one contract:
be predictive, stable, cheap. A question is a measurable function; its
answers are cells; the ladder is a chain of partitions, each coarser
than the last. Questions are generated BY the data (spectral operator /
probes), not written by hand.

T2 (micro-masking). Each level's fast variation masks the level below;
splitting within the invariant REMOVES the mask rather than averaging
it down — each rung re-normalizes the signal-to-noise landscape.
Abstraction is a tower of invariances, each surviving a wider family of
transformations.

T3 (the geometry thesis). The representation space should be
stratified, hierarchy-shaped, and multi-scale: tree/hyperbolic storage
for hierarchies (trees embed in H^2 with bounded distortion; flat
space pays exponential rent), product metrics per factor, straightened
per-stratum geodesics, flag-variety geometry for beliefs over
organizational structure. Intelligence = the world's own geometry
measured into the representation.

T4 (chaos is structure). Chaotic systems are structured, not random:
attractors, invariant measures, Takens embedding from ONE stream,
Koopman/spectral extraction of slow modes. Chaos is better suited to
the framework than generic non-Gaussian noise because its structure is
exact and low-dimensional; the Gaussian restriction is a proving
ground, not a boundary.

T5 (the recognition/generation duality). Fine->coarse (recognition)
is deterministic and cheap; coarse->fine (generation) requires
sampling the fiber distribution p(d|a). The ladder plus per-level
conditionals is a hierarchical generative model; the coarse level
predicts the DISTRIBUTION of the fine, never the instance, and
H(fine|coarse) is the measurable boundary.

### Mechanisms

M1 (active interrogation). The corrected learning objective: the model
generates its own questions; each answer makes new questions possible
(presupposition chaining); validated answers accumulate as the
representation. Question operators: REFINE, DIFF, PART, FUNC, EXISTS,
RELATE. Knowledge state K = (partitions, validated facts). Generation =
validated substitution along category geodesics.

M2 (the adversarial calibration game). Proposer claims facts with
confidence c; a search-driven adversary (compute budget over
nuisances/holdouts/nulls, not a second learner) tries to break them;
equilibrium yields calibrated confidences and robust facts. Training
discipline per TD-Gammon/AlphaZero: one weight set, role heads,
perspective symmetry, structural stochasticity. All system knobs
(budgets, gaps, thresholds, lambdas) become game objectives tuned by
self-play: the adversary hunts the weakest knob.

### Examples

X1 (the cat that teaches the world). The deepest test of a
representation is not "does it label the cat" but "does it encode that
the world is organized" — kinds exist, parts have functions, functions
are cross-cutting phenomena. From a cat: PART decomposes into
ears/legs; FUNC(ear) discovers listening; EXISTS validates listening
across the world and admits it as a new coordinate; the concept
lattice grows. Tier-2/Tier-3 content: knowledge about the organization
of kinds, not just kinds.

X2 (the double pendulum). Deterministic chaotic world, planted
invariants = energy/momentum. Predicted outcome, written before
running: energy-like slow modes at the top rung, chaotic churn in the
fast band, the unsplit core approximating a conservation law. The
beyond-Gaussian teaser and the HN-ready picture.

### Conventions

C0 (naming for adoption). RUNGS = certified abstraction levels
(headline metric). Abstraction Profile (scaling-law-style curve),
States-per-Rung, Bits-per-Rung, Abstraction Diagonal, NIM
(Nuisance-Invariance Margin), LPG-H (level-matched prediction gain).
One 10-line protocol on any embedding matrix; one standard reporting
block for cross-paper comparison.

# PART II — MATHEMATICS (statements; full proofs in theory/)

### Identification lemma
Prediction + whitening fix content up to an orthogonal transformation
(Lemma 2.1 of the monograph). Gaussian case proved; nonlinear case cited
(LeJEPA). Consequence: partition information must come from
correspondences.

### The invariance argument (B/C/D blocks)
Agreement on matched pairs -> B Delta v = 0; sufficient variation
(M = E[Delta Delta^T] positive definite) -> B = 0; orthogonality kills
C for free; block-wise identifiability: z_inv = A u, z_var = D v.

### Multi-block and sequential theorems
K blocks by iterating the argument over K complementary variation families
(provable now). Sequential filtration: rung k+1 applies the same engine to
rung k's invariant output; slow dynamics close exactly; subspaces nest
(H_1 superset H_2 ...); nesting is by construction, audited against truth.
Recall loss (dropped directions) is unfixable downstream — the one
structural failure mode.

### Spectral head and stability
Difference moments, matrix Bernstein, Davis-Kahan: rung k stable when
n_k >= C d log d / gamma_k^2. Gap-selected block counts (Move 4).
Descending eigenvalue order; variant = top d_k; invariant = complement.

### Coarsening contract
The minimal cross-level loss equals H(coarse|fine); zero iff the coarse
partition refines... (coarse is recoverable from fine for free; the
reverse is impossible and is the definition of coarsening).

### Geometry
Stratified product metric with information weights; per-stratum
straightening (Gramian condition-number theorem: eps-straight ->
kappa_eff <= kappa(B)^2 e^{6 eps K}); flag manifold for beliefs over
hierarchies; quasi-isometry requirements for faithful embedding.

### Stop criteria
Single ladder: S1 variation exhaustion (rank test, certificate-grade),
S2 gap collapse, S3 unrepairable audit failure, S4 diminishing compression,
S5 depth safety. Multi-ladder: level-0 domain split by transition
statistics; independent per-domain stops; cross-domain core-merge test
(canonical angles vs permutation null); global stop = forest complete.

## REPORTING.md — Standard Reporting Format for All Stages

Every stage run, control, and ablation MUST produce a report in this format.
The purpose: anyone reading the report can decide pass/fail without asking
what anything means. Formats below follow the SplitJEPA paper
(arXiv:2610.12349) so our numbers are directly comparable to theirs.

---

### A. Gate table (the decision)

One row per frozen criterion from the stage's config file. Nothing else is a gate.

| Criterion | Plain meaning | Threshold | Measured (mean +/- std over seeds) | Pass? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Rules: thresholds come from the frozen config only. Anything not in the
config is labeled "informational, not a gate" in a separate table.

### B. Paper-standard metrics (mandatory for every synthetic run)

These six are the paper's accepted metric set (paper Sec 4.1, App C.1).
Definitions are fixed; report all six even if only some are gates.

| Metric | Definition (plain words) | Paper reference value |
|---|---|---|
| Global R^2 | How well a linear readout recovers the COMPLETE true latent from the representation (all methods share the frozen encoder, so this is roughly constant: 0.9977 +/- 0.0016) | ~0.998 |
| Invariant R^2 | Linear readout from the recovered invariant block to the true invariant factors | >= 0.99 at convergence (their script: 0.9980) |
| Variant R^2 | Linear readout from the recovered variant block to the true variant factors | >= 0.99 at convergence |
| Leakage ||A12||_F (normalized /sqrt(d_H)) | How much variant content leaks INTO the invariant block; report normalized form as the paper does for dimension comparisons | ~0.003-0.006 at 30k pairs (harder mixing: 0.0056 +/- 0.0007) |
| Leakage ||A21||_F (normalized /sqrt(d_L)) | How much invariant content leaks into the variant block; the paper's data shows THIS is the sensitive one: 0.156 at 250 pairs, 0.012 at 30k pairs; grows with dimension (0.0053 at d=4 to 0.0325 at d=64) | ~0.012-0.023 at 30k pairs |
| Block purity | 1 - (||A12||^2 + ||A21||^2)/||A||^2 ; 1.0 = perfect separation | near 1.0 |

Reference anchors from the paper (use these to judge "how far from target"):
- Full method at default setting: leakage ~0.003, block R^2 ~0.998.
- WITHOUT the spectral head (= LeJEPA baseline, same frozen encoder):
  invariant/variant R^2 drop and leakage rises to 0.332 +/- 0.215.
  This 0.33 number is the paper's own ablation (Fig 5b-c) — if our no-head
  baseline shows ~0.33, we are exactly reproducing their ablation.
- Coupling-strength sensitivity (their Table 4): at strength 2.0,
  Global R^2 falls to 0.8765 and leakage rises — this is the published
  picture of "encoder missed the identifiable class".
- Efficiency (their Fig 12): time-to-target 3.16 +/- 1.14 s vs V3's
  8.54 +/- 3.05 s; observations 0.307M vs 1.946M; 64 vs 272 parameters.

### C. Standard visual panel (the fixed figures, same every stage)

Every report includes these figures. Names are fixed so you learn them once.

- P1. Gate summary chart — bars for each gated metric with the threshold
  drawn as a horizontal line. (The fastest "are we green" picture.)
- P2. Difference-moment spectrum — eigenvalues of the pair-difference matrix
  in log scale, descending, with the selected block boundary and gap ratio
  annotated. Success looks like: a clear cliff (paper-style gap ratios >10;
  the paper's head-fitting uses exactly this matrix). Failure looks like:
  a flat line (shuffled-pair control) or a cliff at the wrong position
  (rank-deficient variation).
- P3. Ablation bars — four panels in the paper's Fig 5 style:
  full / w-o predictive / w-o invariance / w-o variance. Expected signature
  per the paper: removing predictive hurts Global R^2; removing invariance
  keeps Global R^2 but destroys the blocks (leakage ~0.33); removing
  variance raises covariance error.
- P4. Scaling curves — line plots vs budget, paper Figs 7-11 style:
  x = samples (or steps), y = each metric, our method vs no-head baseline
  vs shuffled control on the same axes. Success looks like: our curves
  flat-and-high; shuffled flat-and-bad; no-head high on Global R^2 but bad
  on block metrics.
- P5. Ladder diagram (our extension; the paper has no equivalent) — the
  nested subspaces: rung-1 variant | rung-2 variant | ... | invariant core,
  with angles and captures annotated. Success looks like: nested boxes with
  angle < 5 deg at every boundary.
- P6. (Robotics stages only) Block-validation bars in the paper's Fig 14
  style: normalized response of r_H vs r_L under viewpoint and trajectory
  pairs. Paper targets: r_L responds 8.4x/14.0x more than r_H to viewpoint,
  2.1x/3.1x to trajectory.

### D. Verdict and controls

- One plain-language verdict line, e.g.:
  "B1 PASSED on 3 seeds: rung-1 angle 0.8-1.2 deg, rung-2 1.3-1.5 deg,
  both inside 5 deg; category core 0.98; gaps 40-53; controls behaved."
- Controls section: each destructive control with its REQUIRED outcome and
  its observed outcome. A gate pass without behaving controls is NOT a pass.
- Informational metrics (not gates) go in a clearly separated table.

### E. Glossary rule

Any term not in the frozen config or this file gets one sentence of plain
definition at first use, flagged "(addition)". No silent new concepts.

### F. Where results live

Raw JSON per run in results/<stage>/<condition>/<seed>.json, the rendered
report as results/<stage>/REPORT.md, one line appended to docs/STATUS.md.
Never overwrite; failed runs are kept and labeled.

## DEPTH_METRICS.md — Measuring Abstraction Depth (community edition)

Design goals for adoption (checked against the naming standard that made
"transformer" and "scaling laws" stick):
  ONE headline metric with a plain, memorable name.
  Computable on ANY representation (embeddings + any correspondence
  source: augmentations / time / views). No architecture access needed.
  Directly comparable across models, in one line, on one figure.

=====================================================================
THE HEADLINE METRIC:  RUNGS
=====================================================================
RUNGS = the number of certified abstraction levels in a representation.
A level is certified when it passes five audits: identification (leakage
at the permutation null), compression (strictly fewer bits than the
level below), predictive sufficiency, nesting (inside the level above),
and coarsening (the coarser level is recoverable from the finer one).

How to say it:  "Frozen DINOv2 on this dataset: 2 rungs.
                 Our ladder on video: 4 rungs."
One number, any model, directly comparable. This is the abstractness
depth the field has not had a measure for.

=====================================================================
THE SUPPORTING CAST (plain names, one line each)
=====================================================================
Abstraction Profile   The curve: level on the x-axis, certified depth on
                      the y-axis (states, modes, probe-peaks overlaid),
                      saturating where the world's depth ends. The
                      "scaling-law figure" for abstraction. Compare
                      models by profile SHAPE, summarize by Rungs.

States-per-Rung       The sequence of effective state counts per level:
                      exp(entropy of level codes). "240 -> 20 -> 6".
                      Self-explanatory; the geometric answer to "how much
                      of the space does each level cover".

Bits-per-Rung         Entropy of each level; the compression sequence.
                      (Already used throughout this repo.)

Abstraction Diagonal  Probes for labels of increasing abstractness
                      against every level; the accuracy matrix's diagonal
                      is where each concept lives. Diagonal purity =
                      fraction of labels peaking at their planted level.
                      The functional fingerprint of depth.

Slow Modes            Eigenvalues of the transition/difference operator
                      above 1-eps; the plateau count = spectral depth.

Distance Range        Largest-over-smallest resolvable distance per
                      level; its log-decay is the geometric depth rate.

=====================================================================
MINIMUM ADOPTION PROTOCOL (10 lines, any embedding matrix X)
=====================================================================
  1. choose a correspondence source (augmentations / time / views)
  2. whiten X
  3. form pair differences; M = mean of outer products
  4. eigendecompose; take the top block; gap-ratio selects its size
  5. project out; repeat on the remainder -> RUNGS counted with audits
  6. report: Rungs + Abstraction Profile + States-per-Rung
Optional labels add the Abstraction Diagonal. That is the entire
requirement -- no model internals, no gradients, no architecture access.

=====================================================================
STANDARD REPORTING BLOCK (model card, comparable across papers)
=====================================================================
  Model | Dataset | Correspondence source | RUNGS | States-per-Rung
        | Bits-per-Rung | Diagonal purity | NIM | (link to profile)
Calibrate first on synthetic worlds with planted depth (predictions
E28-E34 registered before reporting on real data).

## B2 — Construction Stage: Reporting, Tests, and Targets

B2 asks one question: can rung-(k+1) correspondence pairs be BUILT from the
rung-k representation alone (no ground-truth access), and does the split still
identify the deeper block? The paper always built pairs from ground truth, so
nothing here is replicated from the literature. End-to-end gates are kept
identical to B1 for comparability; everything about construction quality is
new and defined below.

### What B2 must prove

The pipeline  cluster(rung-1 invariant content) -> pairs within clusters ->
rung-2 split  recovers identity at B1-level quality on clean worlds,
degrades gracefully as clustering degrades (following our own contamination
bound), and fails LOUDLY (never silently) when construction is invalid.

### World (frozen design choice)

6 categories, each a tight cluster (mixture of Gaussians; dynamics stay
Gaussian within each kind). Per category: 20 identities, 2 textures each.
The category-separation parameter sigma_cat is sweepable — this is what makes
the degradation study possible.

### New construction-quality metrics (mandatory, measurable in-synthetic)

| Metric | Plain meaning | How measured |
|---|---|---|
| Cluster purity | Fraction of cluster members whose true category matches the cluster majority | ground truth, evaluation only |
| Pair validity | Fraction of constructed pairs that truly share category | ground truth, evaluation only |
| Cluster count vs true | Discovered cluster count (6?) vs planted 6 | cluster labels |
| Misgroup rate | 1 - purity, enters the contamination bound | derived |

### Gates (identical to B1 — frozen)

Each split recovers its block within 5 deg; variant/invariant R^2 > 0.9;
gap ratio > 10; nesting < 5 deg; rank check passes. 3 seeds.

### Targets specific to B2

T1. CONSTRUCTION COST: on the clean world, B2's end-to-end metrics sit within
    2x of B1's angles per seed (B1 with ground-truth pairs, same seeds/world).
    Headline number: the delta B2 - B1 per metric, reported per seed.
T2. PURITY THRESHOLD: with purity >= 0.9 and pair validity >= 0.95, all gates
    pass. (If purity < 0.9 and gates still pass, record it — that is a
    stronger-than-expected robustness result, not a failure of the target.)
T3. GRACEFUL DEGRADATION: sweep sigma_cat so purity runs 1.0 -> 0.7. Expected
    signature (from the contamination bound, monograph Sec. 10): rung-2 angle
    and ||A21|| grow smoothly with misgroup rate; no cliff above purity 0.7;
    the measured leakage stays BELOW the bound's prediction. If the bound
    holds empirically, that is a verification of our own theory — report it
    as a result in itself.
T4. LOUD FAILURE: each control below must fail in its predicted way.

### Controls (each with required failure signature)

C1. Cross-cluster pairs (pairs from DIFFERENT clusters): the rank/validity
    checks must fire; the split must not identify identity.
C2. Clusters built from the VARIANT block instead of the invariant one:
    must fail — construction must use the invariant content.
C3. Singleton clusters (one instance per cluster, no valid pairs):
    rank check must fire.
C4. (Recorded, no required outcome) construction with shuffled pair
    membership within clusters.

### Reporting format (extends docs/REPORTING.md)

Section A: gate table — identical to B1.
Section B: the six paper-standard metrics (required for comparability).
Section C: construction-quality table — purity, pair validity, cluster
           count, misgroup rate, per seed.
Section D: HEADLINE TABLE — B1 vs B2 side by side per metric per seed,
           with the delta column. This is the single table that answers
           "what did construction cost us?"
Figures (fixed panel for B2):
  P2'  difference-moment spectra, B1 vs B2 overlaid on one plot.
  P7   cluster picture — 2-D projection of rung-1 invariant content,
       points colored by true category and by cluster assignment; success
       looks like colors matching clusters.
  P8   degradation curves — x = purity (1.0 -> 0.7), y = rung-2 angle and
       ||A21||, with the contamination-bound prediction drawn as a line and
       measured points overlaid. Success = points under the line, smooth.
  P5   ladder diagram with B2's angles annotated beside B1's.
Verdict line template:
  "B2 PASSED/FAILED: construction cost = <delta summary>; purity <value>;
   degradation followed/did not follow the bound; controls C1-C3 failed
   as required."

### What B2 success does and does not establish

Success: the pipeline is VIABLE without ground truth on synthetic worlds, at
a measurable, small cost relative to B1, with predictable degradation.
It does NOT establish real-world viability (real categories are messier than
6 tight clusters) — that is a later stage with natural data. It also does
not need to BEAT B1 anywhere; matching gates with a small reported delta is
the pass condition.

## STOP_CRITERIA.md — Termination for Single and Multi-Ladder Splitting

A ladder must stop for a REASON, never by convention. Every stop reason is
a measurable condition with a derived threshold, so "the ladder stopped"
is itself a reportable, falsifiable result.

=====================================================================
PART 1 — SINGLE-LADDER STOP (within one world / one video stream)
=====================================================================
Rungs proceed k = 1, 2, ... After each rung's split, evaluate the stop
conditions IN THIS ORDER; the first that fires terminates the ladder:

S1  VARIATION EXHAUSTION (primary; the theorem's condition).
    On the surviving content H_{k-1}, consider every available candidate
    correspondence family. If NONE has full-rank variation,
        lambda_min(Sigma_hat_Delta) <= c(n),
        c(n) = B^2 sqrt(2 log(1/delta) / n)      (Sec 10.2, derivation doc)
    then no further split is IDENTIFIABLE: the remaining content is
    invariant to everything the data knows how to vary. TERMINATE;
    H_{k-1} is the INVARIANT CORE. This is the intended, informative stop.

S2  SPECTRAL GAP COLLAPSE (structural).
    If the best available block split has gap ratio
        lambda_{d} / lambda_{d+1} < gamma_min,
    the world's structure does not support another clean cut (Move 4).
    TERMINATE; report the collapsed spectrum -- it is diagnostic of a
    world whose remaining factors are entangled (predicted by E3).

S3  AUDIT FAILURE, UNREPAIRABLE (epistemic).
    If the rung fails a gate and the failure mode is one the known
    repairs cannot fix (rank deficiency not cured by budget = recall
    loss; see nesting brief), TERMINATE WITH REPORT. Never propagate a
    broken rung downward.

S4  DIMINISHING COMPRESSION (informational; saturation, C3).
    If the compression gained at this rung falls below a bit budget,
        H(rung k) - H(rung k-1) < eps,
    abstraction has stopped paying. TERMINATE (saturation signature).

S5  DEPTH SAFETY (resource).
    k = L_max (e.g. 8). TERMINATE. A backstop, never the expected reason;
    if S5 fires on synthetic worlds, the world design is wrong.

Output: rungs 1..k-1, invariant core, the FIRED STOP CONDITION (one of
S1-S5, with its measured statistic). "Stopped by S1 with a clean rank
test" is a theorem-grade certificate: the world has no more exposed
structure for this correspondence inventory.

=====================================================================
PART 2 — MULTI-LADDER STOP (across worlds / video sources / domains)
=====================================================================
Key fact: one difference-moment operator per DOMAIN. Mixing non-stationary
data in a single operator blends their geometry (assumption A-MARKOV).
So the full system is a FOREST of ladders, with a domain level above them.

LEVEL 0 — DOMAIN SPLIT (itself a split).
    Cluster the data by TRANSITION STATISTICS (not just feature
    similarity): segments with distinct dynamics get distinct domains.
    This is a level-0 partition D_1 .. D_K. (Estimator: same spectral
    machinery on inter-segment difference moments; stop by S1-S2.)

PER-DOMAIN LADDERS (independent stops).
    Each D_j runs its own ladder with Part 1's criteria. Ladders stop
    independently; budgets allocated per ladder by its measured spectral
    gaps (Davis-Kahan: small gaps get more pairs). A domain with little
    structure terminates early and cheaply -- that is correct behavior,
    not failure.

CROSS-DOMAIN PASS — WHEN DO LADDERS MERGE?
    The informative question: do two domains share a deeper world?
    Test the invariant cores: principal/canonical angles between
    core(D_j) and core(D_j'). If aligned beyond a permutation null
    (chance alignment of independent cores), the domains share deeper
    structure -- MERGE the cores into one shared invariant, and record
    the alignment as a cross-cutting phenomenon (the EXISTS machinery at
    the meta-level). Examples: two cameras on one scene (cores align);
    two video sources obeying one physics (cores align at the
    dynamics level); independent worlds (cores fail the null).

GLOBAL STOP.
    All per-domain ladders terminated (each reporting its fired
    condition) AND the cross-domain merge pass complete. The final
    artifact is a FOREST: domain-level partition, per-domain rung
    stacks, shared cores where the test fired, and every termination
    reason on record.

=====================================================================
PART 3 — REGISTRY PREDICTIONS (register before running)
=====================================================================
E35 Single-ladder: on worlds with L planted levels, S1 fires exactly at
    rung L (rank test clean), per seed.
E36 Multi-world: two views of one world -> cores merge (angles below
    null); two independent worlds -> no merge. Both directions.
E37 Budget allocation by measured gaps outperforms uniform allocation
    at fixed total pairs (final angles lower).
E38 Domain split at level 0 recovers planted domain counts.

## PAPER 1 PLAN — Iterative SplitJEPA vs. SplitJEPA: measurable improvements

Framing (honest and stronger than "far better"): SplitJEPA never claimed
depth. Our claim is DOMINANCE BY EXTENSION: at depth 1 we reproduce the
paper exactly (rung 1 IS SplitJEPA); at depth 2+ the base method has no
output at all, and on tasks both can attempt, the ladder wins measurably.
Everything matched: same encoders, same data, same pair budgets, same seeds.

=====================================================================
WORLDS
=====================================================================
W1  paper-style 2-block world  -> PARITY CHECK (ladder rung 1 vs their
                                   Table 1; differences within seed noise)
W2  3-block world (texture/identity/category, continuous texture) -> DELTA
W3  5-block world -> depth-3/4 demonstration (Package-1 acceptance run
                   extended with baseline arms)

=====================================================================
ARMS (all matched)
=====================================================================

A4  Shuffled-ladder control (components, scrambled pairs)

METRICS
=====================================================================

### 1. THE PLUG-IN (SplitHead): the ladder on ANY open-source encoder

Design principle: the encoder stays frozen; the plug-in is pure linear
algebra on top of it, so it attaches to anything in minutes.

    splithead attach <encoder> --pairs <source> [--ladder L]

Pipeline (all unsupervised, no labels, no ground truth):
1. FROZEN encoder (DINOv2, V-JEPA 2, CLIP, SAM, any ResNet/ViT).
2. CORRESPONDENCES from data structure, per domain:
   - images: two augmented views of the SAME image
     (share content, vary color/crop/viewpoint) — universal, free
   - video: adjacent frames / tracked objects (share identity, vary pose)
   - multi-view: same scene, different cameras
3. WHITEN features (ZCA) — as validated throughout.
4. DIFFERENCE-MOMENT spectral head -> variant/invariant blocks.
5. PURIFICATION (Tier 1) — real pairs are noisy; this is the safety net.
6. OPTIONAL LADDER: iterate with constructed pairs per level (B2 track).
7. OUTPUT: block-structured features + per-level linear predictors.

## World-Model Landscape and Where the Ladder Sits

The ladder is one organizing layer with a single insertion pattern. This
document draws the main world-model architectures and marks the insertion
point in each. Two integration modes everywhere: ANALYSIS (frozen encoder,
post-hoc, certified blocks) and COMPILED (sleep-phase; blocks baked into
weights, encoder emits them directly).

=====================================================================
1. AUTOREGRESSIVE TOKEN WORLD MODEL (the LLM)
=====================================================================
    tokens -> [ transformer x N ] -> next-token distribution
                    |  single flat space, single timescale (L=1)

    THE LADDER'S POSITION: analysis layer on hidden states.
    tokens -> transformer -> hidden states X -> [LADDER] -> blocks
    Correspondences are weakest here (no free views/moments): paraphrase
    and consistency pairs. This is the hardest host and the least
    mature; vision first, language later. Flat softmax = the L=1
    special case the ladder generalizes.

=====================================================================
2. RECONSTRUCTION WORLD MODEL (VAE / Dreamer-style)
=====================================================================
    obs -> encoder -> z -> DECODER -> obs (reconstruction)
                       -> dynamics p(z'|z,a) -> planner

    THE LADDER'S POSITION: inside z, decoder-free.
    Replace "z is whatever the decoder needs" with certified blocks:
    z = [z_texture | z_identity | z_core]; dynamics per block
    (block-diagonal transition = independent mechanisms); planner
    reads goals at their level. The decoder, if kept, is demoted to
    an optional renderer per stratum.

=====================================================================
3. JEPA FAMILY (V-JEPA / H-JEPA / LeJEPA / SplitJEPA) -- NATURAL HOST
=====================================================================
    obs -> encoder -> z;  predictor f: z_t -> z_{t+1} (latent, no decoder)

    SplitJEPA adds: correspondences + invariance -> TWO blocks.
    THE LADDER'S POSITION: rung 2, 3, ... of the same engine.
    H-JEPA's hand-set timescale levels BECOME identified rungs:
    their "higher level discards fast detail" becomes a theorem
    (correspondence + sufficient variation) instead of an emergent hope.
    LeJEPA's identification gives the orthogonal-freedom starting point;
    the ladder is what consumes that freedom level by level.

=====================================================================
4. DIFFUSION WORLD MODEL (video simulators: Sora-class, LTX-Video)
=====================================================================
    noise x_T -> [denoise x N] -> clean video; trajectory = granularities

    THE LADDER'S POSITION: on the denoising trajectory's features.
    The noising schedule is already a granularity ladder (high noise =
    coarse structure). The ladder converts it from a sampling artifact
    into organized coordinates: slow/core content extracted at the
    coarse end, detail at the fine end; blocks make the trajectory's
    levels addressable for control, not just generation.

=====================================================================
5. MEMORY WORLD MODEL (Memory Mosaics)
=====================================================================
    contextual memories (kernel regression) + persistent memories

    THE LADDER'S POSITION: blocks become memories. Each rung's content
    is a persistent memory group; predictive disentanglement (their
    demonstrated mechanism) becomes the training pressure that FILLS
    the rungs; the ladder supplies the nesting and the certificates
    the memory view lacks. Substrate for the compiled mode.

=====================================================================
6. RECURRENT / STATE-SPACE WORLD MODEL (Mamba/S4-class, RWKV)
=====================================================================
    obs -> [state h_t = A h_{t-1} + B x_t] -> head

    THE LADDER'S POSITION: eigendecomposition of the learned A.
    The eigenmodes of a linear state transition ARE a slow-fast ladder;
    the ladder's machinery (moments, gaps, rungs) is the tool that
    finds, names, and audits them. Koopman view: the ladder is the
    data-driven route to linearized multi-scale state coordinates.

=====================================================================
7. MBRL PLANNING STACK (DreamerV3 / TD-MPC2)
=====================================================================
    encoder -> latent -> dynamics -> planner (CEM/MPPI) -> actor

    THE LADDER'S POSITION: block-structured latent + telescoping planner.
    Plan in the core (long horizon, cheap), hand subgoals down rung by
    rung; the coarsening contract guarantees each interface. This is
    where the efficiency gains (compute telescoping) are realized.

=====================================================================
THE UNIFIED INSERTION PATTERN (all of the above)
=====================================================================
    data --(correspondences from structure: views/moments/augmentations)
         --> frozen-or-trained encoder --> features X
         --> [ whiten -> difference moments -> spectral split -> rungs
               -> nesting (hard projection) -> purification ]
         --> blocks [z_v | z_1 | ... | z_core] + per-level predictors
         --> consumers: probes / planners / decoders / memories

    What the ladder ADDS to every host, uniformly:
    (a) certified blocks (measured leakage, not assumed),
    (b) a hierarchy with enforced nesting,
    (c) per-level readout (task reads the level it needs),
    (d) audits (gap, rank, angles) that report input quality honestly.

## DECISION_POLICY.md — The exact ask-or-decide procedure (binding)

Purpose: remove judgment from the ask/decide boundary. An agent following
this procedure must reach the same action as any other agent. Prose rules
in AGENTS.md are superseded by this procedure where they differ.

=====================================================================
STEP 0 — DOCUMENT CONSULTATION (always, before anything else)
=====================================================================
Search, in order: docs/DECISIONS.md -> the stage's frozen spec ->
docs/REPORTING.md -> docs/STATUS.md (past precedents).
Set COVERED = true iff an entry answers THIS question as posed
(same objects, same conditions). "Similar" is not covered.

=====================================================================
STEP 1 — COMPUTE THE FIVE TRIGGERS (exact definitions)
=====================================================================
For the question at hand, evaluate:

T1 CRITERION-TOUCH
    true iff ANY option would: change a number/word in a frozen spec or
    config; change the definition of a gate metric; change what counts as
    pass/fail; add or remove a gate.
T2 COST
    true iff the most expensive option exceeds EITHER 1.0 GPU-hour OR
    30 minutes wall-clock, INCLUDING downstream reruns it would force.
    If the cost is uncertain and plausibly above the limit, T2 = true.
T3 IRREVERSIBILITY
    true iff any option cannot be undone by `git revert` with results/
    and docs/ left byte-identical. (A run whose artifacts are kept is
    reversible; deleting or rewriting past artifacts is not.)
T4 RECORD-RISK
    true iff any option writes into results/, rewrites any doc, or
    changes code paths used by a previously logged run.
T5 CONFLICT
    true iff any option contradicts a standing decision or a frozen spec,
    including the case where two applicable entries in DECISIONS.md
    disagree with each other.

=====================================================================
STEP 2 — THE DECISION (deterministic)
=====================================================================
IF COVERED:
    -> DECIDE. Apply the covering entry exactly. Log DECIDE-C.
ELIF (not COVERED) AND (T1 OR T2 OR T3 OR T4 OR T5):
    -> ASK. Add to the checkpoint question batch (format in Step 3).
ELSE:
    -> DECIDE. Choose the default by the rubric in Step 4. Log DECIDE-D.

There is no third outcome. "Unsure" is not an outcome; evaluate the
triggers as written. Missing information for a trigger counts as
trigger-true (conservative).

=====================================================================
STEP 3 — ASK FORMAT (when Step 2 says ASK; one batch per checkpoint)
=====================================================================
Each item, exactly:
    Q:        <one sentence>
    Options:  <the real options, one line each>
    Recommend:<one option + one-line why>
    Impact:   <per option: what changes, one line>
    Reversible: <yes/no per option>
    Cost:     <GPU-hours + wall-clock per option>
    On-silence: <what the default-rubric choice will be if no reply is
                received by the next checkpoint; work does not block>
A question missing any field is not sendable. Work continues on other
fronts under the On-silence default; nothing halts for an answer.

=====================================================================
STEP 4 — DEFAULT RUBRIC (when Step 2 says DECIDE and nothing covers it)
=====================================================================
Apply in order; take the FIRST applicable rule:
    R1  DECISIONS.md / stage spec states a default for this class -> use it.
    R2  The SplitJEPA paper or its released code does it one way -> their way.
    R3  Minimize delta: choose the option closest to what already ran
        (keeps comparability with validated baselines).
    R4  Gate-conservative: choose the option LESS likely to help pass the
        gates (never pick the option that makes passing easier).
    R5  Simplest: fewest new concepts/metrics.
    R6  Arbitrary tie-break: alphabetical; flag as arbitrary in the log;
        add as a candidate entry to DECISIONS.md for human promotion.
Never reorder the rules. Never skip to R4-R6 if R1-R3 apply.

=====================================================================
STEP 5 — LOGGING (every decision, self-made or covered)
=====================================================================
Self-decisions log exactly one line each:
    DECIDE <yyyymmdd-n> | <choice in 12 words or fewer> | rule <Rn or D#> |
           triggers-clear: <T1..T5 as 00000/11111 pattern> | <date>
All DECIDE lines for a run appear in the report appendix "Decisions taken".
Questions asked appear in "Questions escalated" with the human's answer.

=====================================================================
STEP 6 — AUDIT METRICS (human reviews per stage; the agent computes them)
=====================================================================
A. Reversal rate: fraction of DECIDE lines the human reverses on review.
   Target: trending to zero across stages.
B. Avoidable asks: fraction of ASK items that were actually COVERED.
   Each one means a doc entry was too vague -> sharpen it.
C. Arbitrary tie-breaks (R6 count): each should become a D-entry.
D. Gate-drift: any DECIDE that later affected a gate outcome must be
   flagged and reviewed before the stage verdict is recorded.
A stage is not "reported" until A-D are computed and appended.

## FIX BRIEF — Gap-ratio collapse under constructed pairs (B2)

Date: 2026-10-13
Status: fix implemented (pending) -> confirmation run queued
Related: docs/B2.md (stage spec), docs/DECISIONS.md, docs/EVIDENCE.md (P10a-c)

### Symptom
B2 rung-2 gap ratio 3.1 +/- 0.8 vs B1's 27.5 +/- 4.6 on the clean world
(3 seeds). Gate (> 10) failed for B2; recovery metrics (angles, R^2) stayed
near B1. Gate failure is therefore about sharpness, not correctness.

### Diagnosis (mechanism)
Misgrouped pairs (misgroup rate ~0.17, from cluster purity 0.83) cross
category boundaries. Their difference vectors carry energy in the category
directions -- the directions that should be nearly silent at rung 2. This
inflates the eigenvalues just below the spectral cut (lambda_5 region of the
pair-difference moment), flattening the cliff:
    M = M_matched + rho * M_cross,   rho = misgroup rate
    lambda_5 ~ lambda_cat + rho * v_cat
Gap is the most direct numerical fingerprint of pair contamination.

### Fix decision (evidence-ranked)
Tier 1 (DEFAULT ON): purification loop. Score each pair by its
difference-energy in the bottom eigendirections (the would-be invariant
core); down-weight/drop high-energy pairs; re-estimate; iterate to
stability. No ground truth needed; the contamination signature detects
itself. Expected: recovers most of the cliff.
Tier 2 (root cause, parallel track): fix the clusterer (finds 5 of 6
categories); more identities per category; purity >= 0.9. Bias term;
budget alone cannot fix it.
Tier 3 (available, inactive): background subtraction, M_clean ~ M - rho_hat
* M_cross, with M_cross estimated from cross-cluster pairs (C1 control
signature). Needs a misgroup-rate estimate; Tier 1 preferred.
Tier 4 (ALWAYS ON): within-H1 re-whitening before the cut, making the gap
dimensionless and comparable across worlds.

### Open item for human decision (not agent's)
Gate vs informational: recovery was fine at gap 3.1, so threshold > 10 may
be stricter than the theory requires. Decide BEFORE the next B2 run; until
then the gate stands as frozen. Candidate D10.

### Pre-registered predictions (P10a-c, docs/EVIDENCE.md)
P10a: at fixed purity 0.83, Tier-1 purification lifts rung-2 gap from ~3
      toward >= 10 (>= 60% of the B1-B2 deficit recovered).
P10b: lambda_5 ~ lambda_cat + rho * v_cat fits; gap-vs-purity follows the
      predicted curve; purification curve sits above it.
P10c: angles/R^2 move little under purification (sharpness improves,
      position holds) -- confirming sharpness/correctness separation.

### Confirmation run (queued)
B2 clean world, purity 0.83, 3 seeds, Tier 1 + Tier 4 on. Report per
REPORTING.md plus: gap-vs-purity curve, purification curve, P10a-c outcomes.
Results under results/stage_b2_gapfix/ (new directory; no overwrites).

### After the run, record here
- measured gaps (before/after purification), P10a-c verdicts
- clusterer root-cause findings (Tier 2 track)
- human ruling on the gate-vs-informational question -> D10 or unchanged

## FIX BRIEF — Nesting angle above floor (B1/B2 worlds)

Date: 2026-10-13
Status: analysis complete; enforcement implemented (D9); confirmation E9 run
        and refuted; follow-ups open (E10, E11)
Related: docs/DECISIONS.md D9; docs/EVIDENCE.md E9-E11; STATUS.md 2026-10-13

### Symptom
Nesting angle vs absolute truth ~10 deg (both arms, B2 world); gate (<5 deg)
missed. B1/B2 nesting gap ~0. Texture R^2, rung-2 gap, leakage also off
(separate briefs/entries).

### Laboratory findings (executed in-session, linear-Gaussian world)
1. INHERITANCE: nesting ~ theta(H1, H1_true) at every imperfection level
   (~90-100% inherited). Clean-input control: rung-2 intrinsic error ~0.4 deg.
2. BUDGET: inherited error scales as n^{-1/2} (1.37 -> 0.18 deg over
   5k..320k pairs) FOR ISOTROPIC NOISE.
3. SOFT vs HARD, three regimes:
   - isotropic encoder noise: hard wins at every level; all intermediate
     lambda strictly worse.
   - systematic contamination (foreign dir inside H1, truth still inside):
     hard wins (0.04-0.20 deg vs 0.41+).
   - recall loss (true dir dropped from H1): NOTHING works (hard caps at
     the missing angle; soft worse; lambda small enough to reach outside
     floods texture). Unfixable downstream.
4. REAL-WORLD CHECK (E9 run): doubling rung-1 budget moved rung-1 angle
   < 0.02 deg/seed -> on the B2 world the inherited error is ENCODER BIAS
   (budget-independent), not variance. E9 refuted as worded.

### Fix decision (registered as D9)
- HARD PROJECTION = default (project rung-(k+1) block onto rung-k invariant
  subspace before output). Free, exact, dominant in all tested regimes.
- SOFT enforcement (eigendecomp of M - lambda(I-P)): IMPLEMENTED, INACTIVE.
  Activation condition: regime where true target structure measurably lies
  outside estimated H_k AND is reachable at a lambda that keeps faster-block
  contamination out. Not observed in any regime constructed to date.
- Rung-1 quality = the real nesting control: budget (n^{-1/2}, confirmed),
  rank completeness (identity count), gap (texture variation).

### Open follow-ups (registered)
- E10: nesting gate must declare its reference definition BEFORE the run;
  two current definitions disagree by ~12 deg (2.82 vs 10.3). Candidate
  gate: relative nesting <= inherited floor + tolerance. HUMAN RULING.
- E11: lower the rung-1 encoder floor (better/longer training). Budget can
  only help AFTER the bias floor falls. CHECK FIRST: texture discreteness
  (2 discrete values for a 4-dim block under-determines it - then fix the
  WORLD, not the encoder).

### Consequence
Nesting vs ABSOLUTE truth is an encoder-quality diagnostic, not a mechanism
failure: hard projection enforces hierarchy exactly where defined (gap vs
recovered H1 ~0); recovery metrics unaffected at 10 deg (cos 10 = 0.985);
inherited bias passes through at CONSTANT magnitude (no depth cascade).
